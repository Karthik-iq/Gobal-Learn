About GlobalPath Education

Bridging ambitious students with the world's most prestigious academic institutions.

Our Journey

Twelve Years of Opening Global Doors for Tomorrow's Leaders

Founded in 2014 by Oxford and Columbia alumni, GlobalPath Education emerged from a clear vision: international education consulting needed ethical honesty, verified data, and relentless student advocacy.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a boutique counseling firm in New York into a global advisory with direct university partnerships across 800+ accredited institutions in 24 countries.

Our Mission

To democratize access to elite overseas education by providing transparent counseling, eliminating hidden barriers, maximizing financial aid, and empowering students to become ethical global citizens and innovators.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted international education consultancy, recognized universally by students and universities alike for integrity, academic excellence, and life-changing student outcomes.
